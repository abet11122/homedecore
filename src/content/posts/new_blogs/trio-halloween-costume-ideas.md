A trio Halloween costume works when all three members have distinct, recognizable characters that form a clear set. The ideas below are organized by style and theme.

Assign characters based on personality and physical characteristics, not just preference. The most convincing trio costumes are the ones where the casting feels right.

## Classic Trios

### 1. The Sanderson Sisters (Hocus Pocus)

Winifred in green with red hair, Sarah in purple with blonde hair, Mary in red and black with dark hair. This is one of the most popular trio costumes for women and is instantly recognizable.

The wigs are the most important element — each sister has a very specific hairstyle.

### 2. The Three Musketeers

Three people in 17th-century French military outfits — plumed hats, capes, and swords. This is a classic literary trio costume that suits three friends with a sense of adventure.

The plumed hats are the most important element.

### 3. Powerpuff Girls

Blossom in pink, Bubbles in blue, Buttercup in green. Each character wears a simple dress in their color with a black stripe at the hem. This is a simple, recognizable trio costume.

The hair is the most important element — each character has a very specific hairstyle.

### 4. Charlie's Angels

Three women in 1970s-inspired outfits — flared trousers, fitted tops, and big hair. This is a stylish, recognizable trio costume that suits three women who want to look good.

The big, voluminous hair is the most important element.

### 5. The Three Witches (Macbeth)

Three women in dark, tattered robes with wild hair and dramatic makeup. This is a theatrical, literary trio costume that suits three friends with a dramatic flair.

The wild hair and the dramatic makeup are the most important elements.

### 6. Harry, Ron, and Hermione

Harry in a Gryffindor robe with round glasses and a lightning bolt scar; Ron in a Gryffindor robe with red hair; Hermione in a Gryffindor robe with bushy hair and a book. This is a recognizable pop culture trio costume.

The Gryffindor robes are the most important element — they are what makes the trio recognizable as a set.

### 7. The Powerpuff Girls (Live Action)

Blossom in a pink dress with a red bow; Bubbles in a blue dress with pigtails; Buttercup in a green dress with a short dark bob. This is a live-action version of the classic animated trio.

The hair is the most important element — each character has a very specific hairstyle.

### 8. The Plastics (Mean Girls)

Regina George in a pink outfit with a crown; Gretchen Wieners in a pink outfit with big hair; Karen Smith in a pink outfit with a slightly confused expression. This is a recognizable pop culture trio costume.

The pink outfits are the most important element — all three characters wear pink on Wednesdays.

### 9. The Schuyler Sisters (Hamilton)

Angelica in a blue colonial dress; Eliza in a pink colonial dress; Peggy in a yellow colonial dress. This is a musical theatre trio costume that suits three friends who are Hamilton fans.

The colonial dresses are the most important element.

### 10. The Halliwell Sisters (Charmed)

Prue in a dark, sophisticated outfit; Piper in a casual, warm outfit; Phoebe in a bohemian, colorful outfit. This is a recognizable pop culture trio costume for fans of the original series.

The contrasting styles of the three sisters are the most important element.

### 11. Rock, Paper, Scissors

One person dressed as a rock (grey, rounded outfit), one as paper (flat, white outfit with lines), one as scissors (silver outfit with scissor blades). This is a classic punny trio costume.

The props are the most important elements — make them large and clearly recognizable.

### 12. Three Blind Mice

Three people in grey mouse costumes with dark glasses and white canes. This is a classic nursery rhyme trio costume that suits three friends with a sense of humor.

The dark glasses and the white canes are the most important elements.

### 13. The Three Little Pigs

Three people in pig costumes — one in a straw hat (straw house), one in a wooden outfit (stick house), one in a brick-patterned outfit (brick house). Add a Big Bad Wolf for a fourth member.

The house materials are the most important element — they distinguish the three pigs.

### 14. Larry, Moe, and Curly (The Three Stooges)

Larry with wild, frizzy hair; Moe with a bowl cut; Curly with a bald head. Each stooge wears a matching suit. This is a classic comedy trio costume.

The hairstyles are the most important element — they are what distinguishes the three stooges.

### 15. The Three Amigos

Three people in matching Mexican charro outfits — white suits with silver embroidery and large sombreros. This is a classic comedy film trio costume.

The matching outfits and the large sombreros are the most important elements.

### 16. Traffic Light

One person in red, one in yellow, one in green. Each person wears a large circle in their color on the front of their outfit. Together they are a traffic light.

The large colored circles are the most important element — they need to be visible from across the room.

### 17. Primary Colors

One person in red, one in yellow, one in blue. Each person wears a large label with their color name. Together they are the primary colors.

Use large, readable labels — the color name needs to be visible from across the room.

### 18. Sun, Moon, and Stars

One person in a yellow sun costume, one in a silver moon costume, one in a star-covered outfit. This is a celestial trio costume that suits three friends with a mystical aesthetic.

The sun rays, the crescent moon shape, and the star pattern are the most important elements.

### 19. Past, Present, and Future

One person in a Victorian outfit (Past), one in a modern outfit (Present), one in a futuristic outfit (Future). Each person wears a label with their time period. This is a clever, conceptual trio costume.

The labels are the most important element — the concept needs to be readable.

### 20. Faith, Hope, and Charity

Three people in outfits representing their virtue — Faith in white with a cross, Hope in green with an anchor, Charity in red with a heart. This is a classic, allegorical trio costume.

The symbols are the most important element — they need to be large and clearly visible.

## Final Thoughts

The best trio Halloween costumes are the ones where all three members are equally committed and the group is instantly recognizable as a set. Assign characters based on personality and physical characteristics, coordinate the execution level, and plan the group photo in advance. A well-executed trio costume is one of the most memorable things you can do on Halloween.
