A bathroom counter has to support a routine, stay hygienic, and remain easy to wipe down. It gets cluttered when every product is left out or when organizers take up more space than they save. These ideas keep the essentials accessible while leaving the sink usable.

## 1. Clear the Counter Before Sorting

Take everything off the vanity and group it by use: morning routine, evening routine, hair care, and occasional products. Discard expired items and return anything that belongs in another room.

## 2. Keep Only Daily Essentials Within Reach

Limit the counter to the products used every day. Put backups, occasional treatments, and travel items in a drawer or cabinet so the surface stays easier to clean.

## 3. Use One Small Tray

A shallow tray groups a few daily items and can be lifted away when wiping the counter. Choose a size that leaves clear space around the faucet and basin.

## 4. Use a Divided Organizer for Makeup

Small compartments keep brushes, lip products, and daily makeup from spreading across the surface. Pick a washable organizer and store it away from splashing water.

## 5. Move Toothbrushes Into a Wall-Mounted Holder

A holder attached to the wall or mirror frees up counter space. Follow the maker's surface instructions, keep brushes separated, and let them air-dry between uses.

## 6. Choose a Soap Dispenser That Fits the Sink

A refillable pump keeps hand soap contained and reduces mismatched bottles. Leave enough room to use the faucet comfortably and wipe around the dispenser.

## 7. Store Cotton Pads in a Lidded Container

A compact, easy-to-clean container keeps cotton pads or swabs together and protected from splashes. Refill it in small batches rather than displaying several packages.

## 8. Use a Narrow Caddy Beside the Basin

A slim caddy can hold the few products shared by the household without taking over the vanity. Measure the narrowest part of the counter and make sure it does not obstruct the sink.

## 9. Add a Small Drawer Unit Only If It Fits

A compact drawer organizer can hold hair ties, skincare, or grooming tools when the vanity has no built-in storage. Check drawer clearance and avoid placing it where it blocks cabinet access.

## 10. Use Vertical Space Above the Counter

A mirror cabinet or a shallow shelf above the vanity can move products off the counter. Check the height and depth so it does not interfere with the faucet or feel awkward to use.

## 11. Give Hair Tools a Heat-Safe Home

Store styling tools in a drawer or heat-resistant holder after they have cooled. Keep cords away from water and follow the tool maker's guidance for storage.

## 12. Put Shared Items in Matching Dispensers

If several people use the same products, a small set of refillable dispensers can reduce bottle clutter. Label them clearly and keep personal products separate when that helps the routine.

## 13. Use a Tiered Organizer for a Wide Vanity

A compact two-level stand uses height instead of spreading products across the counter. Keep the heavier or most-used items on the lower level for stability and easy access.

## 14. Keep Cleaning Supplies Under the Sink

Store a cloth and suitable surface cleaner in a separate, closed caddy below the vanity. Keeping them close makes a quick wipe-down easier without leaving cleaning bottles beside personal care products.

## 15. Reset the Counter Each Evening

Return products to their assigned tray, drawer, or cabinet at the end of the day. A short reset prevents small items from accumulating and keeps the counter ready for the next routine.

Leave enough open surface to use the sink and clean around it. A few well-chosen organizers work better than filling every inch of the vanity.
