Bathroom shelves look best when they have a job. Towels, spare toilet paper, and a small set of daily products can sit alongside a few simple accents, as long as the shelves remain easy to reach and wipe down. Start with the items the room genuinely needs.

## 1. Fold Towels in a Consistent Way

Fold towels to a similar width so the shelf looks calm and the stack stays stable. Keep the towels used most often at the front or on the lowest easy-to-reach shelf.

## 2. Use a Basket for Small Essentials

A woven or washable basket contains small bottles, hair accessories, or spare toiletries. Choose a material that tolerates bathroom humidity and can be cleaned regularly.

## 3. Keep Backups Together

Group spare soap, toothpaste, and toilet rolls in one basket instead of scattering extras across every shelf. Refill the daily-use area as needed rather than storing open packets on display.

## 4. Add One Plant That Suits the Light

A small plant adds life if the bathroom has enough natural light and ventilation. Choose a variety suited to the conditions, or use a simple artificial stem if the room stays dark.

## 5. Use Matching Containers for Refill Products

Matching containers make frequently used items look orderly. Label them clearly, keep original product details accessible, and avoid decanting anything that could be confused with another product.

## 6. Style With a Small Tray

A tray groups a few items on a shelf and makes it easier to move them for cleaning. Keep the grouping small enough that every item can be reached without removing the whole arrangement.

## 7. Leave Some Shelf Space Empty

Negative space makes the shelf easier to use and prevents it from looking crowded. Leave room for a new towel or the item someone is using rather than filling every gap with decor.

## 8. Use Lidded Jars for Cotton Items

Lidded jars can hold cotton rounds or swabs while protecting them from dust and splashes. Place them away from direct steam and choose a size that is easy to refill.

## 9. Put Heavy Items on the Lowest Shelf

Store heavier bottles or folded bath sheets on a lower shelf to reduce the risk of them falling. Use shelf fixings rated for the load and wall type.

## 10. Add a Small Framed Print

A moisture-resistant print can add color to a dry shelf away from the shower. Use a lightweight frame and avoid placing paper art where it will be regularly splashed.

## 11. Use a Narrow Shelf for a Small Bathroom

A shallow shelf above the toilet or beside the vanity can provide storage without projecting far into the room. Check head clearance and keep the edge clear of door swings.

## 12. Arrange Items by Routine

Place morning items together and evening items together, or assign each household member a small basket. The arrangement should make the daily routine faster, not add another sorting task.

## 13. Mix Closed Storage With a Few Open Items

Use baskets or boxes for small packages and display only a few neat towels or bottles. This balances a finished look with the practical need to hide visual clutter.

## 14. Choose a Limited Color Palette

Repeat two or three colors already in the bathroom, such as white, natural wood, and muted green. Consistent colors help mixed-size supplies feel less busy.

## 15. Keep Cleaning Products on a Separate Shelf

If cleaning products need to stay in the bathroom, store them together and away from toiletries. Keep them in their original labeled packaging and out of reach of children.

## 16. Use Hooks Under a Shelf

Hooks beneath a sturdy shelf can hold a hand towel or washcloth without using the shelf surface. Check the shelf construction and keep hanging items from brushing against the sink or floor.

## 17. Make the Layout Easy to Wipe

Leave a small gap between objects and the wall, and avoid delicate pieces that need special care. A shelf that can be dusted and wiped quickly is easier to keep looking good.

Before adding decor, make sure the shelf is secure and suited to the room's humidity. A useful arrangement with a little open space will stay attractive longer than one packed with accessories.
