A kitchen corner can hide useful storage or become the place where pans and packets disappear. The right fix depends on whether the awkward space is inside a cabinet, on a counter, or along an unused wall. Start by measuring the space and deciding what should live there.

## 1. Add a Lazy Susan to a Corner Cabinet

A turntable makes oils, sauces, and jars at the back easier to reach. Measure the shelf and check that the tray clears the cabinet frame before choosing a size.

## 2. Install a Pull-Out Corner System

Swing-out or pull-out shelves bring the contents of a deep base cabinet toward you. Check the cabinet width, hinge position, and door opening; some systems need professional fitting.

## 3. Use a Two-Tier Turntable

Stacked rotating trays make use of vertical cabinet space for smaller items such as spice jars or tea boxes. Keep heavier bottles on the lower level.

## 4. Store Pots and Pans by Frequency

Keep the pans used every day near the front of a corner cabinet and move occasional baking dishes farther back. A simple grouping prevents the deepest space from becoming a random pile.

## 5. Add a Vertical Divider for Lids

A divider keeps pot lids, trays, and chopping boards standing upright. This makes each item visible and avoids lifting a stack to reach the one underneath.

## 6. Put a Shelf Riser in a Corner Cupboard

A low riser creates another level for cups, small bowls, or pantry jars. Choose a stable shelf that fits the cabinet depth and leaves enough headroom for the items above it.

## 7. Use a Corner Drawer Organizer

If the corner has a drawer, a fitted or adjustable organizer can separate utensils and small tools. Measure the drawer at its narrowest point because angled sides reduce usable space.

## 8. Create a Baking Zone

Keep mixing bowls, measuring cups, and baking ingredients together in one corner cabinet near the work surface. Grouping them by task saves steps and keeps the rest of the kitchen cabinets less crowded.

## 9. Put a Small Appliance on a Corner Shelf

A sturdy shelf can hold a lightweight appliance that is used regularly. Confirm the shelf's load rating and keep cords away from the hob, sink, and hot surfaces.

## 10. Try an Appliance Garage

A countertop cabinet with a lift-up or roll-up door hides a toaster or coffee machine while keeping it plugged in. Check appliance ventilation requirements and leave room for heat to escape.

## 11. Use a Narrow Freestanding Corner Shelf

A small open shelf can fit into an unused wall corner for cookbooks, fruit, or a few everyday dishes. Keep its footprint out of the main path and secure tall furniture if children are in the home.

## 12. Hang a Rail on an Empty Wall Corner

A short rail with hooks can hold lightweight utensils or oven mitts. Before drilling, check for wiring and pipes, and choose fixings suited to the wall and the expected load.

## 13. Add Floating Shelves Above a Worktop

One or two shelves can make a bare corner useful for frequently used cups or small jars. Leave enough clearance below for appliances and avoid placing open food near steam or grease.

## 14. Store Dry Goods in Square Containers

Square containers make efficient use of straight-edged shelves and are easier to line up than round jars. Label the contents and keep the original use-by information accessible.

## 15. Make a Coffee or Tea Corner

Group mugs, tea, coffee, and a small tray in one corner so the routine has a defined home. Keep only daily supplies there; store refills elsewhere to prevent the area from spreading.

## 16. Use the Inside of a Cabinet Door

A shallow rack or a few hooks can hold lightweight measuring spoons, clips, or wraps. Check that the door still closes and that stored items do not hit the shelves.

## 17. Keep the Counter Corner Open for Prep

Not every corner needs an organizer. If it is part of the main preparation area, leave it clear or use one washable tray for the few items that belong there.

The best corner storage is easy to reach and sized for the things you actually use. Measure first, group items by task, and leave enough open space to cook and clean comfortably.
