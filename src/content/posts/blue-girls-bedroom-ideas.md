Blue is a wonderful colour for a girl's room and it's badly underused, mostly because of habit rather than taste.

The versions that work best aren't primary blue — they're the softer, greyer, greener blues that pair beautifully with warm neutrals, brass and natural wood.

## The blues worth choosing

**Dusty blue** — grey-blue, soft and slightly faded. The most versatile choice. Calm without being cold, and it works with pink, cream, rust and gold.

**Powder blue** — pale and airy. Lovely for younger girls, and with warm accents it doesn't read as babyish.

**Teal** — blue-green. More energetic, works beautifully with mustard, coral and warm wood.

**Navy** — dramatic and grown-up. Excellent for a teenager, and it makes white bedding look crisp.

**Cornflower** — a mid blue with warmth in it. Cheerful without being primary.

**Shades to sample:** Pale Powder and Borrowed Light (Farrow & Ball), Palladian Blue and Woodlawn Blue (Benjamin Moore), Rainwashed (Sherwin-Williams).

## The pairing that makes blue work

Blue on its own is cool. What stops a blue room feeling cold is warmth beside it.

**Blue and cream** — softer and prettier than blue and white. Warm white makes blue glow; brilliant white makes it icy.

**Blue and blush pink** — the classic. Dusty blue walls with blush bedding is genuinely beautiful and reads as sophisticated rather than sweet.

**Blue and brass** — gold hardware, a brass lamp, brass curtain rod. This one pairing lifts a blue room more than anything else.

**Blue and rattan** — a rattan headboard, wicker baskets, a jute rug. Texture does the warming.

**Teal and mustard** — bolder, and it looks fantastic. Small doses of mustard in cushions and art.

**Blue and warm wood** — oak or cane furniture rather than white painted.

## Layout ideas

### Dusty blue and cream, the safe beauty

Walls in dusty blue, cream bedding with texture, a jute rug, rattan or oak bedside table, brass lamp. Add one blush cushion.

This works from about age six to eighteen and needs nothing changed but the cushions.

### Navy accent wall

Navy behind the bed, warm white elsewhere. White bedding, brass fittings, a patterned rug with navy and rust in it.

Grown-up, dramatic, excellent for a teen.

### Blue and floral

Blue walls with a floral wallpaper on one wall, or blue walls with floral bedding. Blue is the perfect backdrop for florals because it lets them stay soft rather than sugary.

### Teal and warm neutrals

Teal accent wall, cream and tan textiles, wooden furniture, one mustard accent. Modern, warm, and quite different from most girls' rooms.

## Blue without painting

For renters or the commitment-averse:

- Blue bedding as the main colour statement
- Blue curtains — the biggest single block of colour you can add
- A patterned blue rug
- Removable wallpaper on one wall
- A blue-painted dresser or bedside table
- Blue-toned art in matching frames

Curtains and bedding together will make a room read as blue without touching a wall.

## Texture is essential in a blue room

Flat blue walls plus flat blue bedding is where blue rooms feel cold. Layer these in:

- A chunky knit or waffle throw
- Linen curtains
- A jute or wool rug
- Rattan or cane furniture
- A woven basket
- Boucle or textured cushions

Same colour palette, completely different warmth.

## Lighting

Blue absorbs more light than neutrals, so a blue room needs a bit more of it.

**Warm bulbs, 2700K.** Cool white makes blue go grey and lifeless — it's the fastest way to make a beautiful blue room look flat.

Three sources: overhead on a dimmer, a bedside lamp, and a third — a string of warm fairy lights, a floor lamp, or a lit shelf.

## Growing with her

**Younger:** powder or cornflower blue, playful bedding, more colour in the accents.

**Middle:** dusty blue, simpler bedding, a desk, art she chooses.

**Teen:** navy or deep teal, minimal styling, brass and wood, good lighting.

The colour holds throughout. Only the layers change — which is the whole point of choosing a blue with grey in it rather than a bright one.

---

**The combination I'd pick:** dusty blue walls, cream textured bedding, jute rug, rattan bedside table, brass lamp, one blush cushion.

It's soft without being childish, it photographs beautifully, and it will still look right in ten years.
