Rumi from K-Pop Demon Hunters has become one of the most searched Halloween costumes of the year, and it is easy to see why. The look is specific enough to be recognisable but wearable enough to last a full night out. The combination of dragon braids, pink argyle, and combat-ready layering is genuinely striking.

The ideas below cover solo looks, duo options, and full squad builds. Start with the elements that are easiest to source and build outward.

## The Core Elements

### 1. Get the Dragon Braids Right First

Dragon braids are the single most recognisable part of the Rumi look. Two thick braids with a slight wave, secured with dark ribbon or cord, read immediately to anyone who knows the character. Everything else is secondary.

If your hair is too short for braids, clip-in extensions in a matching colour work well and take about twenty minutes to install.

### 2. Pink Argyle Knitwear

Rumi's signature pink argyle vest or sweater is the centrepiece of the costume. Look for dusty pink rather than hot pink — the palette is muted and slightly vintage, not neon. A thrifted argyle vest over a dark long-sleeve top is the most accurate and affordable option.

### 3. Dark Combat Layers Underneath

Under the knitwear, Rumi wears dark, combat-ready layers — a fitted black or charcoal long-sleeve top, dark trousers, and boots with some weight to them. The contrast between the soft pink argyle and the harder underlayers is what makes the look work.

### 4. The Colour Palette

Dusty pink, charcoal, deep burgundy, and off-white. Avoid anything too bright or too clean — the aesthetic is slightly worn-in and battle-ready, not pristine idol.

## Solo Costume Builds

### 5. The Accurate Build

Pink argyle vest, dark fitted long-sleeve, black or charcoal straight-leg trousers, chunky boots, dragon braids, dark ribbon. This is the closest to screen-accurate and requires no sewing.

### 6. The Elevated Build

Commission or buy a custom pink argyle vest with the exact diamond pattern from the show. Pair with tailored dark trousers and leather-look boots. Add a small prop — a charm or pendant that references the show — for the finishing detail.

### 7. The Budget Build

Thrifted argyle knitwear in any pink tone, dark jeans, boots you already own, and clip-in braids. The total cost can be under twenty pounds if you shop secondhand.

### 8. The Warm-Weather Build

For indoor parties or warmer climates, swap the long-sleeve underlayer for a dark fitted tank and keep the argyle vest as the main piece. Add a light dark jacket you can remove.

### 9. The Cold-Weather Build

Layer a dark oversized coat over the full look. Rumi's coat in later episodes is a long charcoal or dark burgundy style — this doubles as a practical outer layer for outdoor events.

## Makeup and Accessories

### 10. The Makeup

Rumi's makeup is clean with one strong element — a deep burgundy or berry lip. Keep the base natural, add a subtle smoky eye in charcoal or taupe, and finish with the dark lip. Avoid heavy contouring.

### 11. The Accessories

Dark cord or ribbon for the braids, a simple silver or dark metal ring or two, and boots with some visual weight. Avoid anything too delicate or sparkly — the aesthetic is grounded, not glamorous.

### 12. The Prop

A small prop referencing the show — a charm, a card, or a handmade badge — adds a layer of detail that fans will notice immediately. Keep it small enough to carry easily.

## Duo and Group Builds

### 13. Rumi and Mira Duo

Rumi in pink argyle and dragon braids; Mira in the show's darker colour palette with her signature hair. The contrast between the two looks makes the duo immediately recognisable.

### 14. Full Demon Hunters Squad

Assign each person one of the main Demon Hunters characters. Rumi is the most visually distinctive, so whoever does Rumi anchors the group. Coordinate the colour palettes so the group reads as a unit.

### 15. Rumi and Civilian Contrast

One person in full Rumi costume, one in a plain civilian outfit from the show's world. The contrast plays well for photos and is easy to execute.

## Variations and Alternatives

### 16. Gender-Swapped Rumi

The argyle vest and dragon braids translate well across genders. Keep the core colour palette and silhouette — the costume reads regardless of who is wearing it.

### 17. Casual Rumi

A pink argyle crewneck (rather than vest), dark jeans, and white trainers is a more casual interpretation that works for daytime events or low-key parties.

### 18. Elevated Rumi

A tailored pink argyle blazer over a dark turtleneck, with wide-leg dark trousers and heeled boots, takes the look in a more fashion-forward direction while keeping the character recognisable.

### 19. Couples Costume with a Non-Fan Partner

If your partner does not know the show, put them in a simple dark outfit with one Demon Hunters-adjacent detail — a dark coat, a charm, a specific colour. You do Rumi in full; they do a supporting character in a simplified version.

### 20. Last-Minute Rumi

Dragon braids, any pink knitwear, dark jeans, dark boots. If you have those four elements, the costume works. The braids are the non-negotiable — everything else can be approximated.

## Final Thoughts

The Rumi costume works because it is specific without being complicated. Get the dragon braids and the pink argyle right and the rest follows naturally. Start with what you already own, fill the gaps with thrifted pieces, and keep the palette muted. The result is one of the most recognisable costumes of the season without requiring a single piece of specialist kit.
